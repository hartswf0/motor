# The River District

The river bends east below the old crossing.
The mill stands beside the eastern bend.
The kiosk sits above the crossing.
The football field lies behind the school.
The drainage channel runs from the market to the river.
The old crossing is a low bridge of concrete and timber.

Miriam runs the kiosk.
Joseph works at the school.
Amara keeps the bakery.
Tomas drives the ferry.
Nadia teaches at the school.
Marta owns the quarry.

Miriam remembers the flood of 2018 differently from Joseph.
Joseph believes the drainage channel made the flooding worse.
Miriam believes the crossing was always too low.
Amara has avoided the mill since her sister disappeared.
Amara has not spoken to Nadia since the funeral.
Nadia has not spoken to Amara since the funeral.
Joseph fears the river.
Miriam wants the crossing raised.
Tomas needs the ferry licence renewed.

The mill has been closed for years, though residents disagree about exactly when.
Children cross the football field after school.
Only Tomas knows that the crossing was raised without permission.
Marta promised Joseph to speak at the council.

Both Amara and Nadia attend the river ceremony every year.
The river ceremony is held at the old crossing.
